# Exercise: Empirical Introduction

## Prompt

Write an introduction for a study investigating whether children
are perceived as less competent than adults in a social cognition
task. The study uses a between-participants design with 120
participants.

## Expected Structure

1. Hook (provocative question or anecdote)
2. Literature context (2-3 relevant findings)
3. Gap identification
4. Research question / hypothesis
5. Brief contribution statement
